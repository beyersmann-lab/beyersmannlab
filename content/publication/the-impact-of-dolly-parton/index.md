abstract: This study estimates children's potential cumulative word exposure from shared book reading (SBR) using books from the Imagination Library, analyzing 60 books distributed in Tamworth (2020-2024) for ages 0–5. Adapting modeling methods from Logan et al. (2019), findings indicate that reading five books daily could expose children to over 3.6 million more words compared to those rarely read to, while a conservative rate of two books per day yields a gap exceeding 1.4 million words, highlighting how high-frequency SBR significantly boosts language exposure.
